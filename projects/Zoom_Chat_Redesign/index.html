Needfinding · 25 respondents

People don't dislike Zoom chat. They barely use it for files.

Ratings of the current chat and file sharing cluster around the neutral midpoint. The open answers explain why: files vanish when the meeting ends, chat is hard to find, and nothing is saved unless you do it by hand.

What the survey said

The clearest signal

72%

agreed that a Save chat history / files button matters. 18 of 25 respondents rated it 4 or 5 out of 5, the strongest agreement in the survey.

Screen sharing was the most used way to show a file (17 of 25), followed by cloud links (10). Only 3 people shared files through Zoom chat itself.

Most respondents were occasional users: 11 used Zoom rarely and 8 monthly, so the designs had to be obvious without practice.

    Final evaluation · 25 participants

    Every statement averaged above 4.3 out of 5

    Participants used the prototype, then rated eight statements from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). The collapsible sidebar scored highest, with no rating below 4.

    12345 · strongly agree

    What worked

    • The collapsible sidebar was the feature people named most often.
    • Colour-coded tabs and the context-aware Save button were well received.
    • The layout and colours were described as succinct and appealing.
    The collapsable side bar seems very useful.Participant 17

    What I would change next

    • Save automatically. Two participants asked for one save button or automatic saving.
    • Drag and drop for sharing files.
    • A transcript switch in Settings.
    • More depth on desktop. One participant found it better suited to mobile.
    It would be good for mobile but is too simple for a desktop application.Participant 9
